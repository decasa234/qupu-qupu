# SEAMO Syllabus — Grades 1–3

Southeast Asian Mathematical Olympiad (Terry Chew Institute / TCIMO; published by SAP).
Based on the Singapore Mathematical Olympiad curriculum, ~40% modified for international level.
Source: official syllabus PDFs in this folder (`SEAMO-syllabus-A.pdf`, `SEAMO-syllabus-B.pdf`).

Levels for grades 1–3: **Paper A = Grades 1 & 2**, **Paper B = Grades 3 & 4** (Paper K =
Kindergarten, optional).

## Paper format (per public sources — verify against a current paper)
- 90 minutes. **Section A**: 10 MCQ × 3 marks · **Section B**: 10 MCQ × 4 marks ·
  **Section C**: 5 short-answer × 6 marks.

## Topics with no direct WMI equivalent (gap concepts to author)
Pigeonhole Principle · Sum of a Number Sequence (arithmetic series) · Number Puzzle /
cryptarithm · Values of Ones Digit (units-digit cycles) · Division & Remainder as first-class
topics · Working Backwards as an explicit archetype.
